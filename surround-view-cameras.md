# Surround View Cameras
The surround view camera system uses front, side, and rear cameras to create a top-down view of your vehicle on the touchscreen. This view helps you maneuver your vehicle in situations like parking or moving out of a tight space. 
## Camera Locations
| Camera type | Location |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Front camera | Front grille |
| Side cameras | Side view mirrors |
| Rear camera  | Rear bumper  |

>[!WARNING]
>The surround view camera system is meant to be a visual aid. Do not use the cameras as a replacement for checking your surroundings and driving with due care and attention. Failure to follow this instruction could result in property damage, personal injury, or death.

# Using the Surround View Camera System
<img align="left" src="https://img.icons8.com/?size=100&id=VyMvT1KlnPw6&format=png&color=000000" alt="Surround View Camera Button" width="50">

<p>Press the camera button on the touchscreen to access the camera display screen. </p>
<br>

You can also access the camera system using the menu on the touchscreen: 
1. Press the Settings button at the bottom of the touchscreen.
2. Press Driver Assistance.
3. Press Surround View Camera System.
4. Switch Surround View Camera System on or off.

## Switching Between Camera Views
<img align="left" width="50" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8eaa1960-a50c-4ccf-908e-1037005c0a94" />

<p>Press the drop-down button on the top of the touchscreen. </p>
<br>
<p>You can switch between the following views: </p>

| Camera view | What it does |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| **Front view** | Provides a view of the area directly in front of your vehicle. |
| **Front wide view** | Provides a widened 180 degree view of the area in front of your vehicle. |
| **Rear View**  | Provides a view of the area directly behind your vehicle.  |
| **Rear wide view** | Provides a widened, 180 degree view of the area behind your vehicle. |
| **Hitch view** | Provides a close-up view of the area around your tow hitch. |
| **Trailer view**  | Provides a view of the trailer behind your vehicle.  |

## Additional Views
Your vehicle may be equipped with specialty camera views.

Use the menu on the touchscreen to switch additional views on and off: 
1. Press the Settings button at the bottom of the touchscreen.
2. Press Driver Assistance.
3. Press Surround View Camera System.
4. Switch the chosen view on or off. 

### Turn Signal Views 
When you use your turn signal, the corresponding camera view appears on the touchscreen to aid you when checking your blind spot. The view disappears after you complete the turn or when you place the stalk back in its original position. 

### Top-Down Truck Bed Views 
This view provides a top-down view of your truck bed to ensure any cargo or tools are secure when driving or while in park.
